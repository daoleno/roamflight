Geometry reprojected in metres, quantized, partitioned and extruded. Rooflines, colours and facades are illustrative. Heights are rounded source attributes when available, otherwise floors × 3.2 m, then a CNBH footprint-interior estimate when supported, else an explicitly illustrative 8 m mass. The covered bridge uses a representative 12 m envelope. Its mapped deck outline is about 17 m across; the published 23 m width exceeds the mapped outline. The two-storey gallery above the deck is illustrative.

Supplemental Places: Overture Maps Foundation, Meta, Microsoft, OpenTravel, Uberall and PinMeTo. CDLA Permissive 2.0 records only. Selected Chinese names with confidence >= 0.8 and no permanent closure recorded. Categories translated for display. coverage.json tabulates the municipal inventory. A source count for building providers covers the download rectangle before municipal clipping.

Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2026). Central 12 km × 12 km ground colour from Sentinel-2 L2A TCI S2B_48RVU_20260905_0_L2A, resampled north-up at 10 m, WebP compressed. Roads and water polygons overlay this texture. The street chart uses a vector-derived background. Terrain and city boundary use the regional atlas; see ../chengdu/NOTICE.txt.

CNBH-10 m: A first Chinese building height at 10 m resolution. Wanben Wu, Fudan University. 2020 observation, April 2023 release. CC BY 4.0. Source and licence: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7923866 and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. UTM 48N native raster tiles X103Y31 and X105Y31. Derived values are rounded medians from valid interior cells on eligible roofs. Only medians rounding to 20 m or above are applied, based on local cross-checks. Source reports nationwide RMSE 6.1 m; local agreement varies by height and date. See coverage.json and docs/CHENGDU.md.
