# Product Direction

## Name

**漫航 · Roamflight** combines roaming and flight. The name leaves room for open
exploration, cities, regions, countries, aircraft and progression. Package delivery
is one activity, not the definition of the entire game.

## Promise

> 驾驶你的飞机，飞越真实世界，在城市、山川与海岸之间发现新的旅程。

> Explore the real world from your own aircraft. Discover new journeys between
> cities, mountains and coastlines.

## Experience

Roamflight should make a player curious about what lies beyond the next coastline
or mountain range. Flying itself should feel satisfying, while discoveries,
destinations and optional objectives provide reasons to continue.

The intended loop is: **choose a destination → fly and discover → complete an
objective → record discoveries and earn rewards → begin another journey**.
Discovery records, rewards and unlocks are planned, not current functionality.

## Principles

1. **Flight first.** Open into a usable flying experience, not a marketing screen.
2. **Recognizable places.** Geography and landmarks should help players recognize
   the world rather than act as generic scenery.
3. **Accessible depth.** Controls should be approachable; mastery can grow through
   routes, conditions, aircraft and challenges rather than compulsory complexity.
4. **Designed defaults.** Offer a polished audiovisual preset. Do not require the
   player to become a graphics technician or sound engineer before enjoying a flight.
5. **Exploration with purpose.** Free flight and structured objectives should coexist.
6. **Honest presentation.** Never present planned city-scale detail, multiplayer,
   progression or airports as already implemented.

## Scales Of Exploration

- **World:** countries, regions, mountain ranges, islands and long routes.
- **City:** recognizable skylines, landmarks, local routes and low-altitude exploration.
- **Pilot:** varied aircraft, skills, discoveries and a personal travel history.

The prototype currently covers the world layer and a simple delivery loop. City
detail and the pilot progression layer require new data, streaming, performance
budgets and game-design work; they are not merely camera settings.

## What This Is Not

Roamflight is not currently a professional flight simulator, a geography exam,
a multiplayer world or a complete delivery-business simulator. Those expectations
should not be implied by the UI or release notes.
